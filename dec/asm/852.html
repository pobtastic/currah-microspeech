00852: Read Workspace
Used by the routines at HandleInterrupt, ParseSpeechString, TestKeysVariable, NormaliseCharacter, ReadBufferPointer, WriteBufferPointer, IsQueueEmpty and EmptyQueue.
Fetch a byte from the speech workspace. The block lives in the page of *P_RAMT, at low byte 87-index.
Input
A Workspace index
Output
A Byte at that index
HL Address of that index
ReadWorkspace 00852 LD HL,(23732) Fetch *P_RAMT.
The slot is 87 minus the index, in the page of *P_RAMT.
00855 LD L,A Calculate the address of this workspace index.
00856 LD A,87
00858 SUB L
00859 LD L,A
00860 LD A,(HL) Fetch the workspace byte.
00861 RET Return.
