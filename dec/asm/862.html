00862: Normalise Character
Used by the routines at ParseSpeechString and ParseAllophoneGroup.
Convert a character to the alphabet of CharacterKeys and SuffixKeys, and set workspace bit 6 for intonation (set for codes below "a").
Input
A Character
Output
A Normalised character
NormaliseCharacter 00862 PUSH HL Stash HL on the stack.
00863 LD B,A Keep the character in B.
00864 XOR A Call ReadWorkspace for the flags byte.
00865 CALL ReadWorkspace
00868 LD A,B Restore the character.
Fold the letter into the CharacterKeys alphabet. "A" and "a" both become 33.
00869 CP 97 Jump to NormaliseCharacter_BelowA if the character is below "a".
00871 JR C,NormaliseCharacter_BelowA
00873 SUB 64 Subtract 64 from a character at or above "a".
00875 RES 6,(HL) Use low intonation and jump to NormaliseCharacter_Done.
00877 JR NormaliseCharacter_Done
NormaliseCharacter_BelowA 00879 SUB 32 Subtract 32 from a character below "a".
00881 SET 6,(HL) Use high intonation.
NormaliseCharacter_Done 00883 POP HL Restore HL from the stack.
00884 RET Return.
