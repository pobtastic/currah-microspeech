03CA: Allophone Name Sums
The sums of the normalised letters of each parenthesised allophone name, searched along with CharacterKeys by ParseAllophoneGroup. The allophone for each sum is at the same index in the second half of AllophoneValues.
Note that "(gg)" and "(ck)" both sum to 4E and "(gg)" finds the "(ck)" entry first, so it gives KK2. The second 4E (the seventh sum) and its GG2 allophone are never reached. Manual Appendix I documents "(gg)" as GG2 (3D) and "(ck)" as KK2 (29), so the ROM gives "(gg)" the "(ck)" sound instead of the manual's GG2; that confirms the unused GG2 entry was meant for "(gg)".
