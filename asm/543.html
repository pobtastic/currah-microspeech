021F: Parse Speech String
Used by the routine at HandleInterrupt.
Expand the contents of s$ into the allophone buffer. A leading "*" or "?" means the string has already been spoken.
Read the length of s$ and ignore an empty string.

0225 JR NZ,ParseSpeechString_Body Return if s$ is empty.

0228 RET Z
